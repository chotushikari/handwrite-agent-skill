## Handwriting

Casual, readable student handwriting with controlled irregularity: varied letter size, naturally inconsistent spacing, subtly uneven baselines, mild slant variation, imperfect hand-drawn marks, and slight pen-pressure variation. Maintain legibility.

## Paper and camera

Subtle paper texture, mild natural bends, soft ambient shadow, slight smartphone perspective. Keep the full page visible.

## Constraints

- No repeated header on continuation pages.
- No coloured highlights.
- No digital fonts, calligraphy, perfect alignment, or polished infographic layout.
- No omitted or invented content.
